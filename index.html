Service reference — Evo Softail

From the 1997–98 Softail Factory Service Manual #99482-98, §1. The example bike is a 1995 Fat Boy — same Evo Softail platform, so the intervals and fluid types carry over, but year-specific capacities are not printed here as 1995 specs. Your manual wins if it disagrees.

Service intervals — mileage-based only

  • Engine oil + filter — first service 500 mi, then every 5,000 mi
  • Transmission fluid — replace every 5,000 mi; 24 oz HD Transmission Lubricant (99892-84)
  • Fork oil (Fat Boy) — replace every 10,000 mi; Type E, 11.5 oz/leg wet
  • Spark plugs — inspect 10,000 mi / replace 20,000 mi; 5R6A, gap 0.038–0.043 in., torque 18–22 ft-lbs
  • Drive belt — inspect every 5,000 mi; deflection 3/8–1/2 in. under 10 lbs, top strand, cold, rider aboard, tightest point
  • Clutch adjustment — every 5,000 mi; lever free play 1/16–1/8 in.
  • Tires — inspect at every 5,000-mi service; replace on condition/wear, not on mileage
  • Brake fluid — inspect only; no replacement interval in the manual

Brakes — DOT 5 silicone ONLY

DOT 5 silicone brake fluid only — never mix with DOT 3/4. Part nos. 99902-77 / 99901-77 (§1, p.1-42). Brake pad minimum thickness 0.0625 in.

Tire pressures — FLSTF, cold

Solo: 36 psi front / 36 psi rear. With passenger: 36 psi front / 40 psi rear.

Tire age — the 5/10 rule

Michelin/Dunlop motorcycle rule, repeated by Harley-Davidson: have a tire professionally inspected every year after 5 years of use, and replace it at 10 years from the date of manufacture — even if the tread looks fine. Read the date off the DOT code on the sidewall (last 4 digits = week + year, e.g. 5107 = week 51 of 2007). This is not the 6-year figure — that one comes from car-tire makers and car owner's manuals. The tire-age clocks on the Due tab apply this rule per position; enter the manufacture dates, front and rear, on the Bikes & Tasks tab to start them.

Key torques

  • Rear axle — 60–65 ft-lbs
  • Front axle — 45–50 ft-lbs
  • Primary cover — 108–120 in-lbs
  • Banjo bolts — 17–22 ft-lbs
  • Spark plugs — 18–22 ft-lbs

Service reference — Sportster XL/XLH (1986–2003)

From the 1998 Sportster Factory Service Manual #99484-98, §1 "Regular Maintenance Intervals — XLH Sportster Models" (p.1-2), corroborated by the 1986–2003 Sportster Service Manual (Clymer), Ch. 3, Table 1. First service at 500 mi in the factory table (Clymer says an initial 1,000 mi). Items marked * are also done prior to storage or annually. Your manual wins if it disagrees.

Service intervals — mileage-based only

  • Engine oil + filter — replace every 5,000 mi *
  • Primary / transmission fluid — replace every 5,000 mi (one shared sump — one job, not the Softail's two fluids)
  • Primary chain — inspect every 5,000 mi
  • Clutch adjustment — every 5,000 mi
  • Drive belt — inspect every 5,000 mi (first inspect 2,500 mi; adjust at the 500-mi first service)
  • Brake fluid — inspect level/condition every 5,000 mi (DOT 5 only) *
  • Tires — inspect pressure and wear every 2,500 mi (and pre-ride); replace on condition/wear, not on mileage
  • Air filter — inspect/clean every 5,000 mi
  • Battery — tighten connections every 5,000 mi; check fluid level every 2,500 mi
  • Fork oil — replace every 10,000 mi
  • Wheel bearings — inspect/lubricate every 10,000 mi *
  • Fuel tank filter screen — inspect every 5,000 mi
  • Throttle cables — lubricate every 5,000 mi
  • Spark plugs — inspect at 5,000 mi, replace every 10,000 mi
  • Steering head bearing adjustment — inspect/lubricate every 5,000 mi
  • Brake pads — inspect linings and discs for wear every 2,500 mi; minimum pad thickness 0.062 in.
  • Rear fork bearings — inspect/lubricate every 5,000 mi *
  • Jiffy stand — lubricate every 5,000 mi

Where the Sportster book disagrees with the Softail book

  • Spark plugs — Sportster: replace every 10,000 mi. Softail: 20,000 mi.
  • Throttle cables — Sportster: lubricate every 5,000 mi. Softail: 10,000 mi.
  • Tires — Sportster: inspect every 2,500 mi. Softail: 5,000 mi.
  • Fuel filter — Sportster: inspect the tank screen every 5,000 mi. Softail: replace the filter every 10,000 mi. Different jobs.
  • Steering head — Sportster: adjust/lubricate every 5,000 mi. Softail: remove, inspect, repack every 20,000 mi. Different jobs.
  • Primary / transmission — Sportster: one shared sump, one job. Softail: two separate fluids.

The auto-fill uses the Sportster numbers on Sportster bikes and the Softail numbers on Softail bikes — never mixed, never silently overwritten.
