Service reference — Evo Softail

From the 1997–98 Softail Factory Service Manual #99482-98, §1. The example bike is a 1995 Fat Boy — same Evo Softail platform, so the intervals and fluid types carry over, but year-specific capacities are not printed here as 1995 specs. Your manual wins if it disagrees.

Service intervals — mileage-based only

  • Engine oil + filter — first service 500 mi, then every 5,000 mi
  • Transmission fluid — replace every 5,000 mi; 24 oz HD Transmission Lubricant (99892-84)
  • Fork oil (Fat Boy) — replace every 10,000 mi; Type E, 11.5 oz/leg wet
  • Spark plugs — inspect 10,000 mi / replace 20,000 mi; 5R6A, gap 0.038–0.043 in., torque 18–22 ft-lbs
  • Drive belt — inspect every 5,000 mi; deflection 3/8–1/2 in. under 10 lbs, top strand, cold, rider aboard, tightest point
  • Clutch adjustment — every 5,000 mi; lever free play 1/16–1/8 in.
  • Tires — inspect at every 5,000-mi service; replace on condition/wear, not on mileage
  • Brake fluid — inspect only; no replacement interval in the manual

Brakes — DOT 5 silicone ONLY

DOT 5 silicone brake fluid only — never mix with DOT 3/4. Part nos. 99902-77 / 99901-77 (§1, p.1-42). Brake pad minimum thickness 0.0625 in.

Tire pressures — FLSTF, cold

Solo: 36 psi front / 36 psi rear. With passenger: 36 psi front / 40 psi rear.

Key torques

  • Rear axle — 60–65 ft-lbs
  • Front axle — 45–50 ft-lbs
  • Primary cover — 108–120 in-lbs
  • Banjo bolts — 17–22 ft-lbs
  • Spark plugs — 18–22 ft-lbs
